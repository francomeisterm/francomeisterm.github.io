Selected work

2026 · Linux kernel

A power-saving bug in the AMD graphics driver

My RX 6600 started logging a kernel warning at high refresh rates. I traced it to a subtraction in the display driver that wrapped around instead of going negative. Above about 243 Hz, that quietly switched off a memory power-saving feature (MALL) on AMD's DCN 3.0 graphics cards. I wrote a patch that falls back to the shortest delay the hardware supports, and documented how the bug behaved from 144 to 360 Hz.

Reviewed by AMD and merged into mainline Linux 7.3-rc4 in September 2026.

2026 · Prople BPO

Cutting down SSH brute-force traffic

Our Linux servers were getting a steady stream of automated login attempts. I went through 37 days of login and firewall logs, grouped the sources by the networks they came from, and blocked the worst of them at the Cisco firewall.

Daily attempts dropped by 39.9%, and no systems were compromised.

2026 · Prople BPO

Sharing public IP addresses with a reverse proxy

To make better use of our public IP addresses, I designed an HAProxy setup that reads the hostname at the start of each encrypted connection and sends it to the right server. This lets several services share one address and be reached through either of our two internet providers.

First phase deployed and verified through change control.
